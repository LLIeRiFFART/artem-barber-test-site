Why we exist

Barbering got fast. We didn't.

Somewhere around 2014 the industry decided a haircut should take 20 minutes and cost whatever a sandwich costs. The results walked around Brooklyn on thousands of heads: uneven fades, line-ups that looked good for a day, beards trimmed like hedges.

VANTA is the opposite bet. Longer appointments, fewer clients per barber per day, a consultation before every cut and a straight razor on every edge. It costs more. It also lasts three times longer — our clients come back on a 3–4 week cycle, not weekly.

Standards

Six rules every barber follows

01

Time matched to the service

A 10-minute add-on gets 10 minutes; a 90-minute combo gets 90. Nobody waits in the chair while the barber finishes someone else.

02

Consult, then cut

Five minutes on growth patterns, cowlicks, how you style it at home and what your week looks like. The best haircut is the one that still works on day 20.

03

Razor on every edge

Trimmers get you close. A straight razor gets you clean. Every line-up, neck and beard edge is finished by hand with a blade — it's slower and it's the only way.

04

Client notes, always

Guard numbers, product used, what you liked, what you didn't. Written down after every visit so your barber — or any barber — can repeat it exactly.

05

Clean beyond code

Barbicide between every client, fresh towels, single-use neck strips and blades. The chair you sit in has been wiped down less than ten minutes ago.

06

Free 7-day retouch

If a line grows out odd or you want the edges tighter, come back within a week. No charge, no questions, no awkwardness.

Timeline

Ten years, nine milestones

2016

Two chairs behind the records

Marcus opens in the back room of an independent Williamsburg record shop. Word spreads by the second month; the waitlist hits three weeks by the sixth.

2017

Leo joins

A straight-razor specialist from Naples via London. Hot Towel Shave goes on the menu and becomes the second most booked service.

2018

142 Bedford Ave

We sign the lease on the current space — 1,800 sq ft, six chairs, original tin ceiling. Opening week is sold out before the paint dries.

2019

The VANTA exam

We write our internal technical standard: 40 points covering fades, scissor work, razor, consultation and hygiene. Every barber passes it before touching a client.

2020

Surviving

Closed for 14 weeks. We sell “pay it forward” cuts online, keep every barber on payroll and reopen with the online booking system you use today.

2022

Twelve chairs

We take the unit next door. Dante builds the texture programme — curls, coils and gray blending become a third of bookings within a year.

2023

VANTA Matte Clay

After two years of testing we release our own product. Medium hold, zero shine, washes out with water. Still the best seller on the back bar.

2024

Barber of the Year, NYC

Marcus wins his third — the first barber in the city to do so. He mentions it exactly zero times in the studio.

2026

48,000 cuts and counting

Same rule on the mirror. Same razor on every edge. New faces on the floor — and, hopefully, yours in the chair.
