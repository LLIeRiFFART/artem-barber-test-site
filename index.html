The studio

Not just shop, it's studio

VANTA started in 2016 as two chairs in the back of a Bedford Ave record shop. Today it's a 12-barber studio, but the rule stayed the same: one client at a time, no double-booking, no rushing the line-up.

Every service gets the time it needs

From a 10-minute add-on to a 90-minute combo, the calendar blocks the full service duration.

Consultation before every service

Head shape, hair growth, your lifestyle. Then we cut. Not the other way around.

Free re-touch within 7 days

If something grows out odd or you want the edges tighter, come back. On the house.
