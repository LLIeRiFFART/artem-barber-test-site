Getting here

Subway, bike, car, ferry

L

Subway

L to Bedford Ave — exit at N 7th, walk 2 minutes north. G to Nassau Ave is a 10-minute walk. From Manhattan, Union Sq to our door is about 15 minutes.

⛴

Ferry & bike

NYC Ferry East River route to North Williamsburg (N 6th St), 6 minutes on foot. Citi Bike dock directly outside; we have a rack for your own bike inside the entrance.

P

Car

Street parking on N 8th and N 9th is metered until 19:00 — usually possible, never guaranteed. The garage at 80 N 9th St is a 3-minute walk and we validate for 1 hour.
